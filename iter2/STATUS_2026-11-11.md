# Iteration 2 status: meeting with Damir, 11 Nov 2026

Everything below is reproducible from this branch (`iter2/README.md` has the commands); numbers are copied from the result files named in brackets.

## 1. What exists

The multi-camera pipeline runs end to end on the OMEN (RTX 3070 laptop GPU):

- **Sender, per camera** (`payloads.py`, the iteration-1 MMSD sender, unchanged): Mask2Former Swin-L Cityscapes segmentation (photo resized to 512x1024 for the model; the label map is sent at 1024x576 as lossless WebP), Canny edge map at full resolution (WebP), BLIP caption. Payload: 55.6 KB per camera on average, against 547.8 KB for the photo (CARLA v2).
- **Coordinator / receiver** (`coordinator.py`): groups one moment's payloads, takes cam1 as the reference camera Rec1, feeds Rec1's edge and segmentation maps to two ControlNets (canny, seg) on Stable Diffusion 1.5 (UniPC, 20 steps, guidance 7.5, seed 42) and fuses the captions of all cameras into one prompt; an IP-Adapter on the supporting cameras is optional. 10.6 s per master view.
- **Analysis** (`analysis.py`): Faster R-CNN on the master view gives counts, classes and positions per case (JSON, CSV).
- **Resolutions:** photos 1920x1080; transmitted segmentation map 1024x576; edge map 1920x1080; generated master view 1024x576.

| Dataset | Cases | Photos | Used for | Ground truth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CARLA v2 | 112 | 504 | first full run | visible counts per camera |
| CARLA v3 | 112 | 504 | main evaluation | id, box and world position of every visible object |
| CARLA v4 | 224 | 1,008 | training data only | as v3 |
| Real-world Astana, 3 synced phones | 366 core triplets | 1,098 | 61 cases evaluated (every 6th); the rest is training data | none: reference = the detector on the original photo |

CARLA v1-v3 and the real-world set are in the team Drive (`Datasets/Simulated`, `Datasets/Real-world`); v4 is so far only on the OMEN. A small sample with payloads and reconstructions is in `demo/`.

## 2. What we measure

As agreed with Damir (9 Nov), what matters is the **number and positions of vehicles in the reference camera's view**. Our main metric:

- **Vehicle recall**: the share of vehicles visible from Rec1 (ground truth: at least 80 px at 960x540) for which the detector finds a box on the reconstruction that overlaps the true box with IoU >= 0.5. We also report precision, the count error per image (MAE) and recall by vehicle size.
- Real-world photos have no ground truth, so there the reference is the same detector run on the original photo.
- This is stricter than the seed paper's 85-89%, which comes from a VLM judge answering yes/no per image pair on whether count and position are preserved. Under our metric, the authors' own receiver on Cityscapes scored 0.65 recall in our iteration-1 reproduction.
- The "51-53%" mentioned on 9 Nov is the average per-image recall against the detector on the original photo (CARLA 0.51-0.56, real-world 0.51-0.53). Against the CARLA v3 ground truth the pooled recall of the default receiver is 0.485.

## 3. Main results

CARLA v3, all 112 cases, against the ground truth (`results/gt_eval_cn07_15.json`, `results/instance_sender.json`):

| | Recall @IoU 0.5 | Precision | Count MAE | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Detector on the original photo (ceiling) | 0.76-0.78 | 0.85 | 1.46 | - |
| Receiver, Rec1 only | 0.446 | 0.63 | 3.17 | 10.8 s |
| Receiver, fused prompt (current default) | 0.485 | 0.63 | 2.71 | 10.8 s |
| Receiver, fused prompt, ControlNet canny 0.7 / seg 1.5 (proposed default) | 0.537 | 0.61 | 2.50 | 10.5 s |
| Sender-side vehicle list (Faster R-CNN ResNet50 at the sender), see 4 | 0.78 | 0.85 | 1.46 | 97 ms at the sender, +68 B per photo |

Paired change in per-case recall, canny 0.7 / seg 1.5 minus default: +0.046 (95% CI +0.019 to +0.074); on cases 61-112, which were not used to choose the setting: +0.047 (+0.013 to +0.080). Recall by size goes 0.07 to 0.14 under 24 px and 0.38 to 0.44 at 24-48 px.

Real-world, 61 cases, against the detector on the original photo, pooled (`compare_runs.py`):

| | Recall | Precision | Vehicles under 24 px | 24-48 px | 48-120 px |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Receiver, current default | 0.469 | 0.76 | 0.17 | 0.17 | 0.79 |
| Receiver, canny 0.7 / seg 1.5 | 0.562 | 0.75 | 0.21 | 0.31 | 0.88 |

Paired change in per-case recall: +0.066 (95% CI +0.030 to +0.099).

Where the receiver loses vehicles (CARLA v3, `loss_breakdown.py`, `gt_eval.py`):

- **Size:** recall 0.07 for vehicles under 24 px, 0.38 at 24-48 px, 0.80 at 48-120 px, 0.98 above 120 px; 61% of the vehicles are under 48 px.
- **Merging:** 62% of the vehicles share one blob of the segmentation map with another vehicle (the map says "car" per pixel, not which car); the receiver finds 0.51 of those against 0.69 of isolated ones.
- **Not the payload:** 96% of the vehicles the detector finds on the original photo are present in the transmitted map (measured on CARLA v2); they are lost when the image is generated.

## 4. What we tried

| Change | Result | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Fused prompt vs Rec1-only prompt | +0.047 recall (paired 95% CI +0.021 to +0.073), but a control prompt with captions from another case gave a similar gain on 24 cases | keep; the gain is mostly prompt length, not information from other cameras |
| IP-Adapter on the supporting cameras (two readings) | no gain; colour-coded maps made it worse | dropped |
| Generation at 1280x704 and 2048x1152 | no gain (2048: -0.045, CI -0.149 to +0.043, 8.5x slower) | keep 1024x576 |
| Redrawing small cars at 4x in windows (`prompt_refine`) | +0.019 recall on all 173 cases (CI +0.007 to +0.032); +0.056 for CARLA vehicles under 48 px | below the +0.05 bar set in advance: optional, off by default |
| Segmenter at larger input sizes | vehicles with no blob stay at 15-16%; blob recall 0.33 to 0.37 at 3.5x sender time | keep 512x1024 |
| Splitting merged blobs with the existing payload (shape, edge map) | recall rises only by cutting cars into pieces (precision 0.08-0.25) | the current payload cannot separate touching cars |
| Receiver hyperparameters | guidance 4, ControlNets off for the last 20% of steps, negative prompt: worse; guidance 12, 40 steps: no change; **ControlNet canny 0.7 / seg 1.5: +0.057 on held-out cases (CI +0.005 to +0.111)** | confirmed on every case: CARLA +0.046 (CI +0.019 to +0.074), real-world +0.066 (CI +0.030 to +0.099); CARLA precision 0.63 to 0.61. Proposed new default (`--cn-scales 0.7,1.5`), not switched yet |
| Ground-plane fusion across cameras (CARLA camera poses) | reaches vehicles hidden from Rec1 (scene recall 0.35 vs at most 0.28), precision 0.28 | not needed for the Rec1-view metric |
| **Sender-side vehicle list** (class + box, about 9 B per vehicle) | Faster R-CNN ResNet50: recall 0.78, precision 0.85, count MAE 1.46, +68 B per photo; Mask2Former instance: recall 0.68-0.70 and 0.75-0.78 of the merged-blob vehicles, +87-93 B | the largest gain we found; changes the payload, so it needs approval |

## 6. Questions for Damir

1. Can the payload carry a small vehicle list (class + box, about 9 bytes per vehicle) next to the segmentation map, edge map and caption? It is the only change that moves recall substantially (0.49 to 0.70-0.78).
2. Is per-vehicle recall at IoU 0.5 plus the count error in the Rec1 view the right success metric? From what vehicle size should a car count?
3. Could you share the VLM-judge prompt from the paper, so we can also report the paper's metric on our data?
4. Are lab GPUs available for fine-tuning the ControlNets? The training crops are ready: 8,620 training and 1,088 validation crops (CARLA v2 and v4, real-world), with CARLA v3 and the 61 evaluated real-world cases held out.
5. We propose switching the receiver default to ControlNet canny 0.7 / seg 1.5, which needs only one flag.

## Caveats

One generation seed; a detector, not people, judges the reconstructions; the ground truth ignores vehicles under 80 px, so precision is understated; timings are from a laptop GPU, not from edge hardware.
