# Iteration 2 findings: multi-camera master view, counts, classes and positions

Date: 2026-10-25. All numbers come from `REPORT.md` (generated by `make_report.py` from the files under `results/`). Network simulation, node failure and packet loss are out of scope: every camera reports, nothing is delayed or lost.

## 1. What was built and run

| Stage | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Edge node | `payloads.py` | The unchanged Iteration 1 sender on every photo: Mask2Former segmentation (512x1024 input, map sent at 1024x576), Canny edge map at full resolution, BLIP caption. Written as lossless WebP / WebP / text. |
| Coordinator | `coordinator.py` | Groups the payloads of one case, takes cam1 as Rec1, feeds Rec1's edge and segmentation maps to two ControlNets on Stable Diffusion 1.5 (UniPC, 20 steps, guidance 7.5, seed 42, 1024x576), fuses the captions into one prompt. Optional IP-Adapter on the supporting cameras. |
| Analysis | `analysis.py` | Faster R-CNN v2 (score >= 0.5) on each master view and on the original photo; per case: counts per class and every detection with class, score and pixel position (master view and 1920x1080 frame). |
| Geometry and fusion | `geom.py`, `geofuse.py` | Blobs in the received segmentation maps, projected to the ground plane through each camera's pose, associated across cameras. Poses come from the CARLA manifest. |
| Scene evaluation | `eval_union.py` | Scores counts, recall, precision and position error in metres against the objects visible from Rec1 and against the union of all cameras. |

Variants: `k1` = Rec1 only with its own caption (the single-view baseline); `prompt_only` = Rec1 anchors plus the fused caption of all cameras; `ipa_seg` / `ipa_recon` = `prompt_only` plus IP-Adapter on the supporting cameras' segmentation maps / own single-view reconstructions. The architecture document does not say what image the IP-Adapter receives, so these are two readings that use only the payloads. `oracle` = the same detector on the original photo, the ceiling for any reconstruction.

Datasets (all 1920x1080, 3-6 cameras per case, cam1 = the manifest's "main" camera):
- CARLA v2: 112 cases, 504 photos. Ground truth = visible counts per camera (an object counts at >= 80 px at 960x540); no object ids.
- CARLA v3: 112 cases, 504 photos, new scenes (seed 21), same generator settings plus the id, kind, pixel box, depth and world position of every visible object.
- Real-world Astana phone triplets: 61 of the 366 core cases (every 6th). No ground truth, no calibration.

Cost on an RTX 3070 laptop (8 GB): 10.6 s per master view at 1024x576 (18.5 s at 1280x704), 5.4 GB GPU peak. Sender per photo: about 0.28 s segmentation, 0.1 s edge map, 0.3 s caption (on that GPU, not on edge hardware). Payload: 55.6 KB per camera on average against 547.8 KB for the photo; the edge map is most of it (segmentation map 3-6 KB, caption about 50 B).

## 2. Results

### 2.1 Single view vs fused prompt (cars = cars, trucks, buses; error against what Rec1 sees)

| | v2 MAE | v2 bias | v3 MAE | v3 bias | vehicle recall (v2) | person recall (v2) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Detector on original photo | 1.55 | -1.02 | 1.48 | -0.93 | - | - |
| Single view (`k1`) | 3.76 | -3.24 | 3.17 | -2.40 | 0.51 | 0.21 |
| Fused prompt (`prompt_only`) | 3.20 | -2.46 | 2.71 | -1.84 | 0.55 | 0.28 |

Ground truth mean: 9.23 cars per photo (v2), 8.19 (v3). Recall is measured against the detector's own output on the original photo. Paired error difference, `prompt_only` minus `k1`: v2 -0.56 (95% CI -0.89 to -0.19), v3 -0.46 (-0.93 to -0.06). The gain reproduces on a second dataset.

### 2.5 Reading the segmentation map directly, and fusing across cameras (v2, 112 cases, against what Rec1 sees)

| Method | Cars MAE | Bias | Within 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rec1 blobs, min area 10 px | 4.84 | -1.09 | 0.30 |
| Rec1 blobs, min area 30 px | 4.27 | -3.05 | 0.27 |
| + objects >= 2 cameras agree on (10 px, gate 2.5 m) | 4.79 | +0.08 | 0.24 |
| + same (30 px, gate 4 m) | 3.78 | -1.67 | 0.34 |
| + every object that projects into Rec1's frame (10 px, 2.5 m) | 10.84 | +9.75 | 0.08 |

People, Rec1 blobs: MAE 2.53 (bias +0.28) at 10 px, 2.14 (bias -0.96) at 30 px; fusion does not help (MAE 2.58). For comparison, people MAE against ground truth on the same cases: `prompt_only` 4.06 (bias -3.87), `k1` 5.35 (-5.24), detector on the original photo 2.91 (+2.52, because it counts people the ground truth excludes). The 30 px / 4 m row was found by sweeping six settings on the same cases it is scored on, so it is optimistic. Pre-chosen settings (10 px, 2.5 m) give 4.79 against 4.84 for Rec1 alone. Counting every object that projects into Rec1's frame fails because projection says nothing about occlusion.

Geometry check: with the standard CARLA pose convention each case's focus point lands inside the image for 504 of 504 cameras (a flipped yaw: 17%). Pedestrian positions backprojected from depth agree with the recorded actor positions to a median 0.12 m (v3 smoke test).

## 3. What the evidence supports

- Under every configuration tested, the generative master view recovers about half of the vehicles and at most a third of the people the detector finds on the original photo (v2 vehicle recall 0.51-0.58, person recall 0.21-0.32; real-world person recall 0.08-0.10).
- The loss happens in generation, not in the payload: 96% of the vehicles are present in the segmentation map.
- The fused prompt beats the single view on two datasets (MAE 3.76 to 3.20, 3.17 to 2.71).
- Neither reading of the IP-Adapter helped; the one that feeds colour-coded maps hurt. Edge strength and resolution gave no reliable gain.
- The generative master view does not recover objects hidden from Rec1 (union recall 0.17 vs 0.16 for the single view), consistent with Rec1's anchors fixing the layout.
- Geometric association across cameras does reach hidden objects (union recall 0.35 for vehicles, 0.41 for pedestrians, against at most 0.28 and 0.16 for any Rec1-frame method), but with precision 0.28 and a count overshoot of +4.8 vehicles and +11.4 pedestrians per case.

## 4. What it does not show

- That the fused prompt's gain comes from multi-camera information. A control with captions from another case gave a similar result on 24 cases (cars MAE 3.92 vs 3.79; person recall 0.28 for both). The real-vs-shuffled difference was not tested formally and the control was not run on v3.
- That the IP-Adapter fusion in the architecture document cannot work. Two readings were tested, only on 24 v2 cases and at scales 0.2-0.5, not on v3.
- That position accuracy differs between methods. The median vehicle error is 1.6-1.8 m for every method including the detector on the original photo, a floor set by the metric (bounding-box centre vs ground-contact point). Parked cars are not actors, so their ground-truth position comes from depth backprojection (16 of 98 car instances in the 3-case v3 smoke test), and two ground-truth actors with NaN positions in the full set were handled the same way.
- That fusion parameters generalise. They were fixed in advance for v3 and not tuned.

## 5. Limitations

- One generation seed (42), one detector. The detector's output, not human labels, is the reference for recall and precision.
- Ground truth counts only objects >= 80 px at 960x540, so precision is understated for every method, and pedestrian counts against ground truth are unreliable.
- Camera poses for the geometric experiments come from the manifest. A deployment, and the real-world phone data, would need a calibration.
- Captions are BLIP's, not the captioner of the seed paper. CARLA scenes differ from real ones; the real-world sample is small and uncalibrated.

## 6. Open question for Prof. Zorbas: classify or reconstruct?

Reading the segmentation map directly (no generation, 10.6 s per image saved) gives car MAE 4.27-4.84 against 3.20 for the generative view, and is closer to ground truth on people (MAE 2.1-2.5, bias near zero at 10 px) than the generative view (`prompt_only` 4.06, which recovers about 28% of the people the detector sees). Neither route is clearly better for counts, and neither reaches the detector's 1.55. The occlusion-free scene that the master-view idea promises is not produced by the generative route as built; it needs spatial information from the other cameras, which in these experiments only the geometric route supplied, at the price of a calibration.

## 7. Possible next experiments

| Option | Expected benefit | Cost and risk |
|---|---|---|
| Raise association precision on v3 (duplicate suppression, size and class priors, tighter gates) | Directly targets the 0.28 precision; uses the new union ground truth; CPU only | Tuned to CARLA; real phones need calibration |
| Hybrid: geometric association for counts and positions, generation only for the picture | Best measured recall of hidden objects | Needs a calibration source for real data (self-calibration from overlapping views is a research task) |
| Trained multi-camera conditioning (the spring plan) | Highest potential | v2 + v3 are only 224 cases; training and evaluation effort |
| Rerun the IP-Adapter readings and the shuffled control on v3 | Closes the two open points in section 4 | About 30-60 minutes of GPU; low expected gain |
